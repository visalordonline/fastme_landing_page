Our mission

Make local opportunity easier to reach.

We believe finding help—and finding customers—should feel more direct, more accessible and more connected to the communities around us.

What we’re working toward

Fastme aims to connect people who need a service with the skilled professionals ready to provide it, using a clear, location-aware experience.

Make connections simpler

Help customers and service providers discover one another without relying only on personal referrals.

Help skills get noticed

Give skilled workers another way to present what they do and reach people seeking their services.

Build with care

Design useful connections around transparency, respect and thoughtful handling of location information.

Our promise

We’re building for everyday needs: a customer looking for a plumber, a family searching for a hairdresser, or a skilled worker ready to grow a local business.

Fastme is a connector, not a substitute for clear communication. Customers and providers should discuss the work, timing and terms directly, and use location sharing only when it is relevant and permitted.

Be part of a more connected neighborhood.
