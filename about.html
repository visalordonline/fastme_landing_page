About Fastme

Bringing local skills closer to home.

Fastme is a service connector designed to help people find skilled workers nearby—and help service providers become easier to discover.

Good work should be easier to find.

Finding dependable help can take time. At the same time, talented local professionals need a better way to reach new customers. Fastme is built to bring both sides together through one location-aware platform.

Customers can explore services such as plumbing, electrical work, painting, phone repairs, grooming, cleaning, repairs and security. Providers can present their skills and connect with people looking for help in their area. Location tools are there to support coordination and should always be used with the user's knowledge and permission.

One platform, two communities.

Fastme is designed to make it easier to get help and easier to grow a service business.

For customers

Discover local service providers, explore the help you need and reach out to arrange the next step.

For professionals

Showcase your skills, help nearby customers find you and build connections beyond word of mouth.

For local communities

Make useful service connections more visible and accessible within the places people live and work.
